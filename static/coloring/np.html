Incredibly, there is a sense in which checking whether three colors suffice for a map is the hardest and most important of all algorithmic questions. This is especially bewildering because the case of two, or four, or any number other than three is easy to check.

If you find a fast method for checking if three colors suffice, the results would be dramatic. It would revolutionize artificial intelligence and perhaps all of science. Waxing poetically, it would let you automate creativity.

We still don't know if such a fast method exists. It is arguably the biggest open question in mathematics. There is a million dollar prize for a solution.
