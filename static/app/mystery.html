Appel and Haken's proved 4 colors are enough for every map. There's also a simple method to check if 2 colors are enough for any given map.

So 2 and 4 are easy. What about 3? Suprisingly, the 3 color case is one of hardest and most important algorithmic questions in computer science.

A fast method for checking if 3 colors are enough for a map seems innocouous (after all, it exists for any number besides three). But it would have enormous consequences -- at the very least breaking all cryptography and revolutionizing artificial intelligence.

To this day, we still don't know if a fast method exists. It is arguably the biggest open question in mathematics.
